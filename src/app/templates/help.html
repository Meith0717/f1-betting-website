Placing Bets

Before each race, predict the top 3 finishers and the fastest lap driver. Bets close when the race starts.

Scoring

  • +1 point per correct driver in the correct position
  • +1 point per correct team in the correct position
  • +1 point for correct fastest lap
  • +1 bonus for a perfect podium (all drivers AND teams correct)

Maximum 8 points per race.

Leaderboard

Your total score is the sum of all race results. Check the home page for the current leaderboard.

Notifications

Enable push notifications on the home page to get alerts for qualifying, race start, and betting deadlines.
