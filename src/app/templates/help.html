🏁 Game Overview

F1 Betting Platform lets you predict race outcomes and compete with other fans. Earn points for correct predictions and climb the leaderboard!

💰 How Betting Works

📝 Placing a Bet

  1. Navigate to the "Betting" section
  2. Select an upcoming race that's open for betting
  3. Predict the top 3 finishers in order
  4. Optionally predict the fastest lap driver
  5. Confirm your bet before the race starts

⏰ Betting Deadlines

  • Betting closes when the race starts
  • Countdown timers show exactly when betting closes
  • Late bets are not accepted

🏆 Scoring System

🥇 Position Predictions

3rd place correct 5 pts Your predicted 3rd place matches actual 3rd

Prediction Points

📊 Leaderboard & Rankings

  • Points accumulate across all races
  • Leaderboard shows top performers
  • Your rank is shown on your dashboard
  • Reset occurs at season start (admin controlled)

💡 Tips & Strategies

  • 📺 Watch qualifying to gauge form
  • 🏎️ Consider team strategies and tire choices
  • 🌧️ Weather conditions can change everything
  • 🏁 Track characteristics favor different drivers
  • 📊 Check past performance at the circuit
  • 🔄 You can edit bets until the deadline
