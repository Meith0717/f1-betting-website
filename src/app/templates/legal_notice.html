About This Application

This is a private, non-commercial F1 betting game. It is a fan-led project built for entertainment, friendly competition, and coding practice. No real money is ever involved.

Access is strictly controlled via a registration password to maintain a private community environment.

The Scoring System

Your goal is to predict the Podium (Top 3) and the Fastest Lap driver. Points are calculated as follows:

Achievement Points
Correct Driver (per podium spot) +1 pt
Correct Team (per podium spot) +1 pt
Correct Fastest Lap Driver +3 pts
Perfect Podium Bonus (All 3 drivers correct) +1 pt
Maximum Possible Score 10 pts
